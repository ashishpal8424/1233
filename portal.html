Document Portal

Your travel paperwork, in your own Google Drive.

Your own Google Drive

Documents are stored in the Drive you connect. Hotline Holidays never holds a shared copy of your files.

Delivered by us, kept by you

Tickets and vouchers we upload appear in your dashboard, in the right folder, marked as uploaded by Admin.

Preview anywhere

Open PDFs, photos, video and audio in your browser, or download and share them with co-travellers.

You stay in control

We can only reach files in the folder this app creates. Disconnect any time; nothing in your Drive is deleted.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google. Choose any Google account. We never see your password.
  2. Connect Google Drive. Approve access to a folder this app creates. You can pick a different Google account for storage.
  3. Receive and upload documents. Tickets and vouchers appear in organised folders. Upload your own too.
  4. Preview, download or share. Everything stays private to you unless you share it.
