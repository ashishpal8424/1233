Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05. Have a lawyer review this text before publishing it.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, Google account ID and profile picture. We also keep your registration date, account status and an activity log of actions you take in the portal (sign-in, uploads, downloads, sharing).

Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive we request the drive.file permission only. It lets this app create and manage the folders and files that the app itself creates in your Drive. It does not give us access to your other Drive files. We read your storage quota to check there is room before uploading.

How we store credentials

Your Drive refresh token is encrypted on our server and never sent to your browser. We never receive or store your Google password.

How we use your data

To provide the document portal, deliver documents you asked us to prepare, keep the service secure, and contact you about your bookings. We do not sell your data.

Our staff

Hotline Holidays administrators can upload documents to your connected Drive and manage the files within the portal folder. They cannot see other files in your Drive.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings. This revokes our access and does not delete any file in your Drive. You can request deletion of your portal account from Settings or by emailing Info@hotlineholidays.com.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
